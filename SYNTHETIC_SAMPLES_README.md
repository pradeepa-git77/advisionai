# AdVision AI — 150 Synthetic Samples

This bundle contains 150 original, programmatically generated advertisement creative images across 15 mixed categories (10 ads/category) and a matching CSV with AD001–AD150 IDs.

## Important data disclosure
- All ad creatives in this sample bundle are generated designs, not real brand advertisements.
- All campaign metrics are synthetic and are NOT verified real-world performance.
- Use this bundle to test uploads, matching, calculations, dashboards, and analysis workflow only.
- Do not present generated metrics as real advertising evidence or real customer results.

## Contents
- `sample_data/ads/AD001.png` ... `AD150.png`
- `sample_data/campaign_metrics_150_synthetic.csv`
- `SYNTHETIC_DATA_NOTICE.txt`

## Metrics
`impressions`, `clicks`, `conversions`, `spend`, and `revenue` are generated test values.
`data_source=synthetic_generated` and `metrics_verified=false` are intentional provenance fields.
